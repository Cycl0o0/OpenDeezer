OpenDeezer is a native, open-source Android client for the Deezer music service.

Sign in using the embedded Deezer login page, then browse favorites, playlists,
charts, artists, Flow and podcasts. Search for music, view synchronized lyrics,
manage the playback queue and control playback from Android's media controls.

Playback is handled locally by the included Go engine. It supports gapless
playback, crossfade, ReplayGain, an equalizer, a sleep timer, selectable audio
quality and OpenDeezer Connect. Accounts with the required Deezer entitlement
can also save tracks to device storage.

The application contains no Google Play Services, proprietary analytics or
advertising SDKs. Its complete Kotlin and Go source code is available at
https://github.com/Cycl0o0/OpenDeezer.

A Deezer account and access to Deezer's proprietary network service are
required. OpenDeezer is not affiliated with or endorsed by Deezer.
