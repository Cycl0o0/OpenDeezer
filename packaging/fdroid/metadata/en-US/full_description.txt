OpenDeezer is an open-source reimplementation of Deezer by Cycl0o0.

Log in once via the embedded web view. Browse liked songs, playlists, charts, artists, search, Flow and podcasts. Every track plays locally: streamed in, Blowfish-decrypted, decoded and played from memory (MP3 or FLAC on HiFi plans).

One Go engine handles login, decrypt, decode and playback. Multiple native front-ends (terminal TUI, macOS, Linux GTK/Qt, Windows, Android, iOS).

Features include gapless playback with crossfade and ReplayGain, synced lyrics, downloads (paid plans), remote control, device selection, sleep timer, and more.

Prebuilt binaries and source at https://github.com/Cycl0o0/OpenDeezer

Note: Requires a Deezer account. Free accounts supported at 128 kbps (ad-supported); paid plans unlock higher quality and downloads. This client is not affiliated with or endorsed by Deezer.
